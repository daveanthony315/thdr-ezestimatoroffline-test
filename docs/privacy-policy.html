Privacy Policy — THDR Estimator

Publisher: The Home Improvement / House DR · Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Contact: thehousedrcny@gmail.com

1. Local On-Device Storage & Zero Cloud Collection

THDR Estimator operates completely offline-first. All app data is stored locally on your device inside the app's private Room SQLite database (thdr_estimator_db). We do not operate remote accounts, collect analytics or advertising identifiers, or transmit your business or client records to external servers.

2. Customer Information

Customer records you enter—including names, company names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, and job notes—remain strictly on your device. Customer details are used only to prefill estimates and proposals or appear inside printable/PDF documents when you choose to generate one.

3. Estimates, Proposals & Business Details

Room dimensions, service scopes, Price Book rates, material takeoffs, taxes, discounts, proposal terms, and your company header details are saved locally on your device. When you tap Print / Save PDF, Android renders the document locally through the system Print Manager; we never receive or store a copy.

4. Data Retention

Your saved estimates, proposals, customer records, Price Book items, and preferences remain stored locally on your device until you edit them, delete them inside the app, reset settings, clear app storage, or uninstall THDR Estimator.

5. Device Backups & Manual Exports

To protect client contact details and pricing proposals, automatic Android cloud backups (allowBackup="false") and automated device-to-device transfers are disabled via Android backup and data extraction rules excluding thdr_estimator_db. Data leaves your device only when you manually export a Price Book JSON backup or export/share a PDF through Android's system file picker or print dialog.

6. Data Deletion & User Control

You have full control over your data at all times: delete individual estimates, proposals, customers, or Price Book items directly in the app; reset customization settings in Settings; or remove all app data permanently via Android Settings > Apps > THDR Estimator > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Contact & Policy Updates

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how THDR Estimator handles data on your device, contact thehousedrcny@gmail.com. Any future policy updates will be reflected inside this screen and at the matching published web URL listed in Settings.
